Custom Insoles

Provided by Hike Medical

Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to millions of different feet. They flatten out fast and were never shaped for you in the first place. Getting truly custom insoles through a specialist means $800+ on average and months of waiting. Hike closes that gap.

Foot pain rarely stops you all at once. It just talks you out of things, one at a time: the walk after dinner, the workout you keep meaning to get back to, weekend plans that used to sound fun. Custom insoles are shaped to your exact foot, so your body can stop working around the problem and go back to doing the things you actually want to do.
